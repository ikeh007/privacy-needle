How this estimate works

A remote-work security budget is easiest to review when identity and MFA, endpoint protection, device management, backups, password management, training and compliance administration are separate lines. Enter actual procurement quotes for accurate decisions.

The preset buttons are illustrative, not vendor bids. Per-user licenses are multiplied by the number of staff and contractors. A fixed compliance platform cost is then added, an assumed discount subtracted, and onboarding/device replacement amounts allocated over time. Do not interpret any regional note as legal advice.

Consider what user information the technologies process. Employee monitoring, device telemetry, international access and software supplier contracts create privacy duties independent of software licenses.

Frequently asked questions

Does selecting Nigeria, the EU or the US change license prices?

No. Vendor pricing depends on contract, tier and location. The region selector only changes the compliance topics to review.

Is the result proof that my organization is compliant?

No. Buying security tools does not by itself establish compliance. Policies, governance, training and incident response require operational work and independent professional review.

Are contractors and employees priced the same?

This simplified model assumes each requires a paid seat. Adjust seat count and prices for limited-access or zero-cost contractor plans.

Does this include payroll and broadband?

No. It models an IT security/compliance stack, not total employee compensation or operating expense.
