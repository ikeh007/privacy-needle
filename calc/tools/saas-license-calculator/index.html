How this estimate works

The model estimates the combined monthly cost of a user-defined number of SaaS tools multiplied by seat count and an average license price. It subtracts any assumed discount and allocates total spending between active use and potential non-use.

Unused allocations are not guaranteed savings. Term commitments, minimum purchases, shared-seat licenses and bundled enterprise contracts may prevent direct recovery. Treat the savings line as a screening opportunity, then confirm vendor-specific termination rules.

Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate wasted SaaS subscriptions?

Use the number of paid seats, price per seat and the share assigned to users who actively need the service. Verify use from audit logs and vendor billing exports.

Is unused spending automatically recoverable?

No. Annual contracts, minimum seats, notice periods and pooled licenses may prevent immediate cancellation.

Does this model cover per-usage and consumption pricing?

No. It is designed for illustrative per-seat license models. Usage-based billing requires a different rate card.

How should procurement teams use this result?

Use it as a shortlist of assumptions to reconcile against contracts, identity provider reports and software owners before changing licenses.
