Privacy & Advertising Notice

How Privacy Needle Calc handles local calculator data, scenario URLs and advertising.

AdvertisementInformation page advertising placement

Who operates the site

Privacy Needle Calc is a free cloud, AI and SaaS cost calculation site developed as part of the Privacy Needle digital product portfolio. Contact info@privacyneedle.com.

Information processed

The calculators process the numbers you enter within your web browser. No server database is required for calculations. Clicking “Save on device” stores a scenario in localStorage on your device. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data controls. Copying a scenario URL places your inputs in that link; anyone with the link can see the values. Do not enter private company secrets, access tokens or personal information in shareable scenarios.

Advertising

Privacy Needle Calc includes advertising placements and the Google AdSense publisher and placement IDs. On regular pages, our ad tags are requested automatically, subject to applicable privacy requirements, your browser settings, Google policies, site approval and ad inventory. Visitors in regions where consent or opt-out messaging is required are served through Google's certified Privacy & messaging consent platform when it is configured and published. Without valid consent where legally required, personalized or other cookie-dependent ads must not be served; Google may instead show eligible limited ads or no ads. This privacy-policy page intentionally does not request third-party advertising. When paid ads cannot load, we promote Privacy Needle's own products.

External links and products

Links to Privacy Needle products and third-party vendors open separate destinations governed by their own privacy notices. We do not control their third-party cookies or accounts.

How to withdraw

Visitors covered by applicable consent rules can use Google's Privacy and cookie settings control to review or withdraw choices where a published Google consent message is available. The Advertising preferences link opens Google's consent settings where supported, otherwise this privacy notice. Local calculator scenarios can be removed by clearing site data. Privacy Needle's first-party promotions may remain visible when third-party advertising is unavailable. Review this notice for compliance with the actual hosting, advertising and analytics setup before launch.
